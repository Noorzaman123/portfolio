Building the Future, One API at a Time

I'm Noor Zaman, a Computer Science graduate from Peshawar, Pakistan with a deep passion for backend development, scalable architectures, and cybersecurity. My journey began with curiosity about how the internet works — and quickly evolved into building production-ready applications.
